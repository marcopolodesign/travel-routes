Maison Felix · 2025

Maison Felix is an exclusive 29-key, 13,569 SF boutique hotel in North Miami Beach, Florida. Conceived as an urban oasis with a nostalgic European flair, the project repurposes a historic 1940s structure, infusing it with distinctive character and a carefully curated thematic identity.
